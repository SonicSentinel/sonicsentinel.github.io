# Sonic Sentinel

Acoustic burst watch for macOS and iOS. It listens to the audio input you choose, shows the spectrum, waterfall and detections, and tells you when something stands out: ultrasound, loud tones, sweeps and the published acoustic signatures. Audio is analysed on your device and is never recorded or sent anywhere.
